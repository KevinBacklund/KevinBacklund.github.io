About Me

Who I am

I am a game programmer who is experienced in C++ and C#, I have mainly worked in Unreal engine and Unity.

I am an problem-solving individual who enjoys understanding how things work beneath the surface. I am fascinated by the technology and systems in games, especially the engines, tools, and underlying architecture that make game development possible.

My goal is to become a skilled Game Programmer specialising in engine and tools programming. I want to develop a deep understanding of the technology behind game development and contribute to building the systems and tools that empower other developers to create games.
